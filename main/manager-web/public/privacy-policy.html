Introduction

Welcome to the Xiaozhi Backend Service (hereinafter referred to as "the Service"). The operator of the Service is the deployer and manager of this service instance (hereinafter referred to as "Operator" or "we"). We understand the importance of your personal information and are committed to protecting its security.

Please read and understand the full content of this Privacy Policy before using the Service. By starting to use the Service, you indicate that you have read and agreed to this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy applies to the collection, storage, use, sharing, and protection of your personal information when you use the Service via the Console, API endpoints, or other methods.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with services, we may collect the following information:

1.1 Account Registration Information: When you register an account, we collect your phone number or username and password to create and verify your account.

1.2 Device Information: When you bind a hardware device, we collect device identifiers (such as device ID, MAC address), device model, firmware version, etc., for device management and service delivery.

1.3 Agent Configuration Information: When you configure an agent, we collect role templates, language model selections, voice parameters, etc., to provide personalized AI interaction services.

1.4 Voice Interaction Data: When you use voice interaction, the service uses Voice Activity Detection (VAD) to detect speech status and processes your audio input, transmitting it to third-party ASR and LLM services for processing.

1.5 Image Data: When using vision features, image data captured by device cameras may be processed and sent to third-party vision model services for analysis and scene understanding.

1.6 Conversation Memory Data: When memory is enabled, the service stores interaction summaries to provide coherent, personalized experiences in subsequent dialogues.

1.7 Knowledge Base Data: When using knowledge base features, uploaded documents and text are stored for retrieval and question answering by the agent.

1.8 Voiceprint Data: When using voiceprint recognition, voiceprint feature samples are collected and stored for speaker verification and personalized services.

1.9 Chat History Data: We store dialogue history with agents, including text content, timestamps, and context, for continuous dialogue and history lookup.

1.10 Dialogue Audio Data: Audio recordings of interactions may be stored to optimize voice interaction quality and support audit queries.

1.11 Agent Configuration Data: Tags, plugin configs, and context provider settings are collected to support agent customization.

1.12 Device Firmware Data: Firmware versions and upgrade history are recorded during OTA upgrades for management and traceability.

1.13 Log Information: Server logs including access time, IP address, browser type, and operation records are automatically collected for operations and security.

1.14 Verification Codes: SMS verification codes are sent for identity verification during login and registration.

2. How We Use Collected Information

The collected information is used for the following purposes:

(1) Providing, maintaining, and improving the Service, including account, device, and agent management.

(2) Processing voice requests via VAD, ASR, LLM, and TTS services.

(3) Processing image data through vision model services for recognition and understanding.

(4) Storing and managing dialogue memory for coherent ongoing interactions.

(5) Storing and retrieving knowledge base content for accurate QA responses.

(6) Ensuring service security and stability, including authentication and troubleshooting.

(7) Complying with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Information is only shared under the following conditions:

3.1 Third-Party Service Providers: Voice, image, and text data may be transmitted to third-party AI service providers (LLM, ASR, TTS, vision) for processing.

3.2 Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by applicable laws, legal processes, or government authorities.

3.3 Security Protection: Using or disclosing information within reasonable necessity to protect the rights, property, or safety of operators, users, or the public.

3.4 Explicit Consent: Sharing information with third parties with your explicit consent.

4. Storage and Protection

4.1 Storage Location: Information is stored on servers deployed by the operator, including databases (MySQL/PostgreSQL) and cache services (Redis).

4.2 Storage Period: Information is retained for the period necessary to provide the service and deleted or anonymized upon account cancellation.

4.3 Security Measures: Technical and organizational measures including encryption and access controls are implemented to protect your data.

4.4 Open Source Notice: In self-hosted deployments, data storage and processing are entirely managed by the deployer.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete your personal information, cancel your account, or withdraw consent at any time via the Console.
