# Nobix Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

TinyBot lives in your home and listens to you, so we treat your information with care. This policy explains what Nobix Industries ("Nobix", "we") collects through the TinyBot robot, the Nobix app and the Nobix web console ("the service"). It also covers why we collect it, how long we keep it and what rights you have.

## 1. What we keep

**Account details.** Your username or mobile number, your password (stored encrypted, so we cannot see it) and the date you joined.

**Robot details.** The robot's ID and MAC address, firmware version, whether it is online, when it was last seen, and its settings (name, look, volume and so on).

**Speech and voice.** The robot sends the audio of what you say to our servers after it hears its wake word ("Hey TinyBot" or another), or when you start a conversation. There, speech is turned into text and an answer is made. The text of the conversation, and the audio if that setting is on, is kept in the chat history.

Wake-word listening happens inside the robot, and nothing before the wake word is sent. To sense that someone is nearby, the robot only detects the sound of speech; that audio is not sent anywhere.

**Voice recognition.** If you choose to enrol someone's voice, a voiceprint is made from a sample of their speech so TinyBot can tell who is talking. Only enrol a person with their consent.

**TinyBot's memory.** After a conversation, TinyBot may note a few lasting facts, such as your name, what you like or your daily habits, so it remembers them next time. You can turn memory on or off, and see or delete each note in the robot settings.

**Your day.** Alarms, reminders, routines, days to remember, and the messages you send the robot.

**Location.** The name and approximate latitude and longitude of the city or area you choose, used for prayer times and weather. We do not track your location with your phone's GPS.

**Robot setup.** Your Wi-Fi network name and password go from the app straight to the robot so it can connect to the internet. We do not keep your Wi-Fi password on our servers.

**Technical data.** Server logs used for security and troubleshooting, such as times, IP addresses and error messages.

## 2. Why we use it

- To understand what you say and have TinyBot answer;
- To run alarms, reminders, prayer times, routines and messages on time;
- To let TinyBot remember earlier conversations (when memory is on);
- To keep accounts secure, prevent fraud and fix problems;
- To update the firmware and the app;
- To comply with the law.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Who we share it with

- **AI providers.** We use outside AI providers to turn speech into text, write answers, and turn text into speech. They receive only what an answer needs: the speech or text, the character description and relevant memories. Their servers may be outside Bangladesh.
- **Hosting.** The company that runs our servers stores the data.
- **SMS.** Your mobile number goes to an SMS provider to send you a verification code.
- **The law.** When Bangladeshi law or a valid court order requires it.

Each of them may use the information only to provide their service to us.

## 4. How long we keep it

- Account details are kept for as long as you have an account.
- Chat history and audio are kept until you delete them, or until the server's retention period ends.
- Memory notes, voiceprints and your day's settings are kept until you delete them.
- When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your information within a reasonable time. We keep only what the law requires us to keep.

## 5. Your rights and controls

- **See and delete:** you can see and delete memories, chat history, voiceprints, reminders and messages in the app or console. "Forget everything" erases all of TinyBot's memories at once.
- **Turn off:** you can keep memory off. When the robot is switched off or disconnected, no speech reaches our servers.
- **Correct:** you can change your account and robot details.
- **Delete your account:** send a request to the contact below.

## 6. Security

Passwords are stored encrypted, sign-in details are sent encrypted, and access to the servers is limited. No system is completely secure, so use a strong password and never share it.

## 7. Children

Nobix accounts are for adults. If children in the family talk to the robot, the account holder is responsible as their guardian. If you do not want to keep a child's voiceprint or memories, delete them or keep memory off.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and announce major changes in the app.
