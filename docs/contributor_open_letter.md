# An Open Letter to Developers

"When spring warms the river, the ducks know first; it is time for the pufferfish to head upstream!"

Dear friend, I am John, a Java programmer at an ordinary company. Today, with sincere enthusiasm, I am writing this open letter to you, someone who loves AI technology and innovation.

Half a year ago, I saw many excellent projects such as `Dify` and `Chat2DB`, and other AI-related projects. I kept thinking: how great would it be if I could contribute to projects like these? Unfortunately, I had nowhere to apply my skills and ended up spending ten years writing code without much direction.

At the beginning of 2025, I came across videos from the Xiaoge team. I was very curious about how they built their system, and I wanted to recreate their backend service to build a low-cost civilian Jarvis. Sadly, what I have built so far is still only an artificial fool: low concurrency, no soul, slow responses, and many bugs.

The Xiaoge team is our benchmark. I would love to have a Xiaozhi backend service as intelligent as theirs. But I also understand their decision not to open source everything. "A single blossom does not make spring; a hundred flowers in bloom fill the garden." In this age of AI blossoming everywhere, perhaps our generation will be the one to realize a low-cost civilian Jarvis with our own hands. I personally believe that what they can achieve, we can also achieve — it is only a matter of time. I call this our "journey to the West."

So what difficulties will we encounter on this journey? I suspect not fewer than eighty-one trials. Along the way there will inevitably be all kinds of demons, of course, but also deities helping us from the shadows, and people joining the pilgrimage.

If the lines above made you laugh, then I feel very fortunate. Being able to make you smile for five seconds out of the more than 30,000 days in your life is also a contribution.

Will this low-cost civilian Jarvis idea fail? I do not know. But is failure in an ordinary life something unusual? Not really.

One thing is certain about the future: someone will absolutely recreate the Xiaoge team's functionality and build a civilian low-cost Jarvis. Will that be us?

I look forward to walking this path with you and creating the future together.

John, 2025-03-11, Guangzhou

# Appendix: Developer Contribution Guide

## Project goals

1. **A low-cost civilian Jarvis solution**
2. **A solution for intelligent integration with surrounding hardware**

## Join us

We warmly welcome like-minded friends to join us and contribute to the project. You can view the features we plan to implement soon at [this link](https://github.com/users/xinnan-tech/projects/3). Any item in the list that has not yet been assigned to someone is in urgent need of your help. There are two ways to participate:

### 1. Become a regular contributor

Fork the project, submit a PR, and after review by the developers, it will be merged into the main branch.

### 2. Become a developer

After you have submitted 3 valid PRs, you can contact the group owner to apply to become a developer. The group owner will invite you to a separate developer group where we discuss the future of the project together.

## Developer workflow

1. **Create a new branch**  
   Each feature should be developed in its own branch. The branch name should be short and clear, making the implemented feature obvious at a glance and avoiding collisions with other work.

2. **Submit a PR for review**  
   After development is complete, submit a PR on GitHub and let other developers review it. Once approved, merge it into the main branch.
